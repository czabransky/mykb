# Tones

In Chinese, the tone of a syllable can change the meaning of a word. There are four main tones in Mandarin Chinese, plus a neutral tone. The tones are:
1. First tone (high level): mā (妈) - "mother"
2. Second tone (rising): má (麻) - "hemp"
3. Third tone (falling-rising): mǎ (马) - "horse"
4. Fourth tone (falling): mà (骂) - "scold"
5. Neutral tone: ma (吗) - a question particle

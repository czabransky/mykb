Nominal Predicates (名词谓语)

A nominal predicate identifies or describes a subject using a noun or adjective without a copula verb like "is" or "are."

The copula (是 shì in Chinese, "is/are/am" in English) is a linking verb that connects the subject to its predicate.

English Pattern
SubjectToday
+
Copulais
+
PredicateMonday

How Chinese Differs​

Chinese often omits the copula 是 (shì) where English requires it, creating more natural expressions.

Omit 是 (More Common)
  • Dates and time expressions
  • Weather and temperature
  • Casual conversation
vs
Include 是 (When Needed)
  • Identity statements
  • Emphasis required
  • Formal writing

Dates and Time​

Natural (Without 是)
今天十二月二十二号
jīntiān shí'èr yuè èrshí'èr hào
Today December 22nd
vs
Emphatic (With 是)
今天是十二月二十二号
jīntiān shì shí'èr yuè èrshí'èr hào
Today IS December 22nd

Examples​

今天星期一
jīntiān xīngqī yī
Today Monday
Natural without 是
今天很热
jīntiān hěn rè
Today very hot
Adjective predicate
现在三点
xiànzài sān diǎn
Now 3 o'clock
Time expression
他是学生
tā shì xuésheng
He is a student
Identity needs 是

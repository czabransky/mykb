## How It Works

- **Center node** (钢琴) is fixed in position
- **Connected nodes** are attracted to their connections
- **All nodes** repel each other to avoid overlap
- **Hover** over any node to see its English meaning
- **Different line styles** indicate relationship types:
  - Solid lines: general relationships
  - Thick colored lines: compound components
  - Dashed lines: synonyms/similar words

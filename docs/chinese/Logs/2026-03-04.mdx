## What Are You Doing?

When asking someone what they are doing in Chinese, you can use the phrase `你在做什么？`.


<CharacterGrid
    characters={[ 
        { char: '你', pinyin: 'nǐ', english: 'you' },
        { char: '在', pinyin: 'zài', english: 'indicates ongoing action' },
        { char: '做', pinyin: 'zuò', english: 'to do' },
        { char: '什么', pinyin: 'shénme', english: 'what' },
    ]}/>
    
    
Answers to this question can vary widely depending on the context. For example, if someone is cooking, they might say `我在做饭` (Wǒ zài zuò fàn) which means "I am cooking". If they are working, they might say `我在工作` (Wǒ zài gōngzuò) which means "I am working". The key is to use the structure of the question and fill in the appropriate verb and object to describe what you are doing.

    example={{
        chinese: '我在做饭呢。',
        pinyin: 'Wǒ zài zuò fàn ne.',
        english: 'I am cooking.'
    }}/>

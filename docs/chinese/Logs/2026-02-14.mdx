## Describing the Weather

When describing the weather in Chinese, we can use the word `天气` (tiānqì) which means "weather". We can then use simple words like `晴` (qíng, sunny/clear), `雨` (yǔ, rain), `雪` (xuě, snow), and `风` (fēng, wind), and combine them with `天` (tiān, day/sky) to form things like `晴天` / `雨天` / `雪天`. We can also use adjectives like `热` (rè) for "hot", `冷` (lěng) for "cold", and `暖和` (nuǎnhuo) for "warm".

For actions, you can use verbs like `下雨` (xiàyǔ, to rain), `下雪` (xiàxuě, to snow), and `刮风` (guāfēng, to be windy). And you can modify adjectives with adverbs like `很` (hěn), `非常` (fēicháng), `有点` (yǒudiǎn), and `太` (tài).

:::info
Notice that these characters of `shénme shíhòu` (when) are not written here separately as `shénme` (what) and `shíhòu` (time), but instead are combined together into a single word. This is because the meaning of `shénme shíhòu` is not just the sum of the meanings of `shénme` and `shíhòu`. Instead, `shénme shíhòu` has a specific meaning of "when", which cannot be easily inferred from the meanings of `shénme` and `shíhòu` alone.
:::
